THINK BEFORE YOU FLUSH
01 / THE ADVOCACY

A little action can protect a whole water system.

We often forget what happens after we flush. The waste does not simply disappear. It travels through a network of pipes and wastewater systems.

Toilets are designed for human waste and toilet paper where appropriate. Throwing other materials into them can cause blockages and interfere with wastewater management.

FLUSH SMART! is an awareness campaign that encourages proper disposal habits and responsible sanitation practices.

01 — PREVENT

Help prevent clogged pipes by keeping inappropriate materials out of toilets.

02 — PROTECT

Support wastewater systems by practicing proper disposal habits.

03 — PRESERVE

Help protect the environment through responsible sanitation.

02 / THE CONSEQUENCES

The problem goes
down the drain.

Improper flushing may seem harmless, but its effects can extend beyond your bathroom.

What goes down the toilet doesn't just disappear.

01 — PIPE BLOCKAGES

Clogged drainage

Wipes, plastics, and other materials can accumulate and contribute to blocked pipes.

02 — WASTEWATER ISSUES

System disruption

Improperly disposed waste can interfere with wastewater collection and treatment.

03 — ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Water pollution risks

Poor waste management can contribute to pollution and threaten the cleanliness of waterways.

03 / KNOW WHAT TO AVOID

NOT EVERYTHING
BELONGS IN HERE.

These everyday items should never be treated as toilet waste. Put them in the proper waste bin instead.

🧻

Wet Wipes

Can remain intact and contribute to pipe blockages.

🩹

Sanitary Products

Can absorb water and cause obstructions.

🪥

Cotton Buds

Do not break down like toilet paper.

🛍️

Plastic Wrappers

Can accumulate in pipes and drainage systems.

04 / THE BETTER WAY

Change your habits.
Change your impact.

Responsible sanitation does not require complicated actions. It starts with simple everyday decisions.

01

Think before flushing

Ask yourself whether the item is actually suitable for toilet disposal.

02

Dispose properly

Use waste bins for wipes, sanitary products, plastics, and other household garbage.

03

Spread awareness

Encourage friends, family, and your community to practice proper sanitation.
